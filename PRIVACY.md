# Privacy policy of Scan SubRead

Scan SubRead has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The
developer gets no data from the app.

## Data on the device

The app keeps these items in its own storage on the device:

- a copy of the last book that you opened,
- the text of the pages that it read,
- the page, the subtitles and the settings of each book.

Uninstalling the app deletes them. "Clear all pages" in the menu deletes
the pages.

## Data that goes to other apps on the device

- The text that you tap goes to the dictionary app that you choose.
- A word and its sentence go to SubRead Anki when you make a card.
- The text of the book goes to SubRead when you make subtitles.
- The app asks SubRead Overlay for the position of the audiobook player.

These apps are on your device. Scan SubRead sends nothing to a server
for them.

## Network

The first OCR run of a language downloads the language data of Tesseract
from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`. The request sends no data about you or your
books. The CDN sees the IP address of the device, as each web server
does. The privacy policy of jsDelivr applies to it:
https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net

The app makes no other network request.
